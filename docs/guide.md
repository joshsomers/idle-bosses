# Idle Bosses guide

The full rules, every boss and every upgrade. For a quick start, see the [README](../README.md).

## How it works

- **Hits:** each XP drop hits the boss you're fighting. Damage is scaled for each skill, so an hour of Runecraft hits
  about as hard as an hour of Agility. Kills take 15 seconds to 4 minutes of normal training.
- **Big bosses:** every boss has its real hitpoints, and tougher ones take bigger hits (the square root of how much
  tougher than Obor they are), so even the Nightmare's 7,800 HP goes down in about 4 minutes of training.
- **No limit:** a big drop, like a lamp or a full Blood Altar craft, hits in full. Damage past a boss's last hitpoint
  carries on into the next boss, so one big drop can kill several in a row, each rolling its own loot.
- **Weaknesses:** each boss takes double damage from one skill, like the Giant Mole from Mining or the Kraken from
  Fishing. The boss list shows that skill's icon by each boss's name.
- **Loot:** every kill rolls the boss's real drop table, with the Wiki's rates, including the pet. Collection log items
  go into the boss's log, and everything adds to your "looted" total at Grand Exchange prices. The sidebar shows the
  kill count each log item dropped at beside its drop rate.
- **Unlocks:** each boss needs a number of collection log slots, filled across all bosses, so you're never stuck behind
  one 1 in 5,000 drop. It also needs some kills of the boss before it, about 10 minutes of fighting it, so you meet each
  boss on the way up. A boss you've fought stays unlocked.
- **Extras:**
  - About 1 kill in 30 brings out a superior, with double health and an extra loot roll.
  - Each kill in a row adds 1% damage, up to 25%. The streak ends after a minute without XP.
  - When a boss's log is complete, the boss turns gold, and its row in the boss list gets a gold border.

Hitpoints and Slayer XP don't hit, because they only come alongside combat XP.

Each sidebar tab opens with a short card on how it works, until you click **Got it**. The **?** by the heading brings
the open tab's card back.

The times are medians from `ProgressionTest`, which plays the ladder 200 times at a typical pace with no weakness
bonus. Each run first gets the kills the next boss needs, then fights the unlocked boss most likely to give a new log
slot. Log slots work like the game: an item fills its slot on every boss whose log it's on, so a dragon pickaxe from
King Black Dragon counts for Kalphite Queen too. Pick which unlocked boss to fight in the Idle Bosses sidebar panel.

Obor's and Bryophyta's log items come from the chest in their lairs, so each kill counts as one chest opening.

Bosses whose loot comes from a chest, waves or a reward pool count each kill as one run with a standard setup: for
example all six Barrows brothers at full reward potential, a Fight Caves or Inferno run off-task, all 12 Colosseum waves,
delves 1 to 8 at Doom of Mokhaiotl, a 750-point Wintertodt game, a 5-permit Tempoross game, and a solo Zalcano. Their
hitpoints are everything you'd kill in that run, so they take longer: about 5 minutes for the Fight Caves and 7 for the
Inferno. `docs/drop-sources.md` lists every setup. The Wintertodt storm is scenery, not an NPC, so it's drawn from its
model.

Each raid kill is one completed raid, using the Wiki money-making guide's setup:
- Chambers of Xeric: solo, 33,000 points.
- Theatre of Blood: normal mode, a trio with no deaths.
- Tombs of Amascut: Expert solo at raid level 300.
- The Fractured Archive: a trio with no deaths.

As in the game, a unique replaces the raid's common loot. Raid hitpoints are the raid's bosses at that team size, so a
raid takes 3 to 7 minutes. The raid shown is its final boss: the Great Olm's head, Verzik Vitur, Tumeken's Warden or
Ocellus.

## Upgrades

Loot adds its Grand Exchange value to your coins, which you spend in the sidebar's Upgrades tab. Your "looted" total
counts everything you've ever looted and doesn't go down when you spend. Saves from before upgrades start with their
looted total as coins.

Some upgrades also need items in your collection log, like the Voidwaker's hilt, blade and gem. The items stay in your
log. No upgrade changes a drop rate. With everything bought, you hit about 70% harder, less than a boss's weakness gives.
Until you have the items, the sidebar lists them in place of the effect; hover over the row to see the effect.

Both come from the bosses' Wiki attributes.

When an effect triggers, a banner across the boss on the HUD names the weapon and what it did for about a second, like
"Dragon claws x4", "Sanguinesti staff +50%" or "Voidwaker Finished". The claws', scythe's and shortbow's hits land a
beat apart, like the real special attacks.

**Cannon**: a dwarf multicannon (25M) fires every 8 ticks (4.8 seconds) while you train, hitting 0 to 8 times the boss's
toughness. That's about a fifth more damage. It holds 30 cannonballs, about 2.5 minutes of fire, then press Reload in the
sidebar, or set a Reload cannon key. Cannonballs cost 150K for 100, about 1.1M an hour of fire. Granite cannonballs cost
400K for 100, hit 20% harder and load first. The cannon and poison stop when you stop training, so nothing is wasted
while you're away.

## Settings

- **Show HUD**: the box with the boss, its health and kill count, your streak and its weakness (lit while you train
  it), the boss's whole collection log with the last kill's new items framed, your cannonballs and your coins.
- **Show boss in HUD**: the animated boss at the top of that box. It's always shown in the sidebar panel too. Both
  are drawn by the plugin from the boss's game model and animations: it idles, flinches when hit, dies and respawns.
- **Walking mini boss** and **Walking mini boss size**: off by default. Also shows a small boss in the game world,
  walking after you along the tiles you've walked. It's only drawn on your screen and can't be clicked.
- **Chat messages**: kill counts, new log items and unlocks. Only you see them.
- **Rare drop sound**: a ding with the level-up fireworks for pets and log items rarer than 1 in 100.
- **Reload cannon**: a key that reloads your cannon. Not set by default.
